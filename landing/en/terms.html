3. The services

Pharnos publishes several distinct services. Each has its own page, its own price and, where relevant, its own delivery method.

3.1 The Pharnos platform (subscription)

A cloud application for managing regulatory dossiers: RIM catalogue, Module 1 tree by country, dossier lifecycle, correspondence with agencies, collaboration between manufacturer and local representative. Access is granted by subscription, currently on invitation. Plans and prices are shown on the pricing section of the home page.

3.2 The CTD Builder (licence)

An application that assembles CTD dossiers on your own machine. Your documents stay on that machine and are never uploaded to our servers. The licence is metered by compilation — the operation that produces the final package — according to the offer purchased.

3.3 Document upgrade (one-off deliverable)

A paid, one-off service: you supply an existing regulatory document, and Pharnos returns it rewritten onto the official template of the country concerned, together with the accompanying files described on the offer page. The deliverable is sent by email once processing is complete.

The Checking Standard (a completeness diagnostic that requires no document upload) and the Regulatory Library (official templates available for download) are provided free of charge, with no sign-up and no purchase obligation. They are provided as they stand, for information purposes.
