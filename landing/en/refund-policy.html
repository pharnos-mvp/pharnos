Refund Policy

If a Pharnos service does not deliver what it promised, we refund it. This page states the guarantees the law gives you, then the rule for each offer, the time limits and the steps to follow — with no small print.

1. The principle

A service that was not delivered, that does not work, or that does not match its description is refunded in full. We do not ask you to justify the request, and we do not deduct any handling fee.

Beyond that principle, the rules below set out the case of each offer, because a monthly subscription, an annual licence and a document delivered within minutes are not the same commitment.

This policy forms an integral part of our Terms of Use and Sale, and applies to every order placed on pharnos.com and its associated services.

2. Your statutory guarantees

The commercial rules set out below are granted in addition to the guarantees the law gives you. They never replace them and never limit them. Where a clause of this page conflicts with a statutory guarantee, the statutory guarantee prevails.

2.1 Legal guarantee of conformity for digital content and services

We must supply digital content and digital services that conform to the contract and to the use that may reasonably be expected of them. If you are a consumer resident in the European Union, this guarantee arises from Directive (EU) 2019/770, transposed in France at articles L. 224-25-12 and following of the Consumer Code.

  • One-off supply — a deliverable, a licence: we are answerable for defects of conformity that appear within two years of the supply. Those appearing within the first twelve months are presumed to have existed at the time of supply, and you do not have to prove it.
  • Continuous supply — a subscription: we are answerable for defects of conformity throughout the whole period of supply.

The remedy comes in a fixed order. You are first entitled to have the content or service brought into conformity, free of charge, within a reasonable time and without major inconvenience to you. Where that is impossible, unreasonably delayed or insufficient, you are entitled to a reduction of the price or to termination of the contract — that is, a refund.

This guarantee costs you nothing. It stands entirely apart from the commercial rules of this page, and no purchase, option or subscription is required to benefit from it.

2.2 Delivery, conformity and hidden defects — commercial sales

For a commercial sale between professionals, we owe an obligation of delivery and an obligation of conformity under the OHADA Uniform Act on general commercial law. Under articles 255 to 259 of that act, you have one month to report an apparent defect and one year to act on a hidden defect.

Consumers in Benin also benefit from the protections of Law No 2007-21 of 16 October 2007 on consumer protection.

2.3 How to invoke a statutory guarantee

Write to contact@pharnos.com describing the defect and giving the order reference. No form and no particular wording are required: naming the problem is enough. We answer within two working days and tell you which remedy we apply and within what time.

3. Right of withdrawal

If you are a consumer resident in the European Union, in the United Kingdom or in any country granting an equivalent right, you have fourteen days from the order to withdraw from it, without giving any reason.

To exercise it, an email to contact@pharnos.com stating your order reference is enough. No particular form is required.

Our services are aimed at professionals, who as a rule do not benefit from this right. We nonetheless apply the fourteen-day window to every customer, professional or not, under the conditions of article 5.

4. Digital content delivered immediately

Some of our offers are delivered within minutes of payment: the document upgrade in particular. For those, you expressly ask for immediate performance at the time of the order, and you acknowledge that the right of withdrawal lapses once delivery is complete. That consent is collected on the payment page, before payment.

The loss of the right of withdrawal changes nothing to the statutory guarantee of conformity of article 2, nor to your right to a refund if the deliverable does not match its description, is unusable, or never reaches you. Article 6 covers those cases, whatever the time elapsed.

5. The rule, offer by offer

Offer Window Condition
Platform subscription 14 days Full refund of the first payment of a new subscription, on request within fourteen days. Afterwards, you may cancel at any time: the subscription is not renewed, and the period already paid for runs to its end without being refunded.
CTD Builder licence 14 days Full refund as long as no compilation has been consumed — the licence counter is the reference. Once a compilation has been produced, the licence has delivered what it was bought for, and articles 2 and 6 alone apply.
Document upgrade Until delivery Full refund at any time before the deliverable is sent. After delivery, the right of withdrawal has lapsed (article 4), but a non-conforming or undelivered order is refunded under articles 2 and 6.
Audit and support services Before work starts Full refund as long as work has not begun. Once begun, the part not yet performed is refunded pro rata.
Free tools and trials Not applicable The Checking Standard, the Regulatory Library and the free trial involve no payment: there is nothing to refund.

6. Always refunded

Whatever the offer and whatever the time elapsed, we refund in full:

  • a payment taken twice, or an amount charged that is higher than the one displayed;
  • an order paid for but never delivered, or a deliverable that did not reach you;
  • a deliverable that does not match the description of the offer, or that is unusable, where bringing it into conformity is impossible or insufficient;
  • a service that a fault on our side prevented you from using for a significant period;
  • an unauthorised payment made from your account, once established.

In these cases, write to us with the order reference and, where relevant, the deliverable concerned: that is all we need.

7. Not refunded

Subject always to the statutory guarantees of article 2, which no clause below may set aside:

  • A decision of a national authority — a dossier declared inadmissible, refused, or an authorisation withheld. Pharnos is a tool, and never guarantees a regulatory outcome (article 5 of the Terms).
  • A subscription period already consumed, or a licence whose compilations have been used.
  • A change of mind after a deliverable has been received and used, where it conforms to its description.
  • An account closed for a breach of article 12 of the Terms.

8. How to request a refund

Write to contact@pharnos.com with the reference of your order — it appears on the receipt sent to you — and one line explaining the request. No form, no telephone call, no procedure to discover.

  • We acknowledge receipt within two working days.
  • We give our decision within five working days of the request.
  • Where the refund is granted, it is issued immediately after that decision.

If your order was placed through Paddle, you may also contact Paddle directly, which handles refunds for the orders it processed.

9. How the refund is paid

A refund is always paid back to the payment method used for the order — the same card, the same mobile money account. We cannot pay it to another method, nor in another currency.

Once issued, the funds usually appear within five to ten working days, depending on your bank or operator. That period is theirs, not ours.

For an order processed by Paddle.com Market Ltd, acting as merchant of record, the refund is issued by Paddle and appears on your statement under its name.

Any tax collected on the order is refunded with it.

10. Chargebacks

If something is wrong with an order, please write to us before disputing the payment with your bank. A chargeback takes weeks, freezes the account concerned and settles nothing that an email would not have settled faster.

We may suspend access to a service that is the subject of a chargeback until the matter is resolved.
