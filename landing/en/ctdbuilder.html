FAQ

The questions that actually get asked

What happens if I change computer?
Export your dossier from the builder, open it on the new machine. The export is a single file containing everything: tree, documents, metadata. If you want that to be automatic, that is exactly what the cloud platform does.
Does it work without an internet connection?
Yes. Assembling, checking and compiling all run on your machine. A connection is only needed to refresh the country reference and to load your compilation credits — which you then use offline.
Where exactly do my documents live?
On your machine, or in the folder you choose — Drive, OneDrive, Dropbox, a network share. If you pick a Drive folder, your files go to Google, not to us. The sentence that is true in every case: your documents never pass through Pharnos servers.
And if my machine dies?
You lose what was not exported. That is the honest counterpart of working offline, and the builder actively reminds you to export. Pointing it at a synchronised folder removes the risk entirely.
Which browsers?
All of them for assembling and compiling. Saving directly into a folder on your disk requires Chrome or Edge; on Safari and Firefox the builder falls back to download and manual export, and says so.
Will there be eCTD?
Yes — on the same foundation, with the same rule: what is assembled on your machine stays on your machine.
