Where you upload dossiers containing personal data of your own staff, patients or third parties, you act as the controller of that data and we act as your processor, under the conditions of article 14 of our Terms.

2. What we collect

What we collect depends entirely on which service you use. Each surface is listed separately, because they are genuinely different.

2.1 This website

Browsing pharnos.com requires no account and no form. Our host records the technical data needed to serve and protect the site (IP address, browser, pages requested). If you request a demonstration or a diagnostic report, we collect the details you type in: name, email address, organisation, country, and your message.

2.2 The Checking Standard

The diagnostic runs in your browser. No document is uploaded, and none is transmitted to us. Only if you ask for the report by email do we collect that email address and the answers needed to produce the report.

2.3 The CTD Builder

Your dossiers live on your machine and nowhere else. We receive only what is needed to operate the licence: the licence key, the compilation counters and the technical data of the check. The content of your documents never reaches our servers.

2.4 The platform (app.pharnos.com)

Account data (name, email address, role, organisation), the data of your dossiers (products, countries, procedures, deadlines, correspondence), the documents you upload, and the activity logs needed for traceability and security.

2.5 Purchases

The order (offer, amount, date, reference), the billing details you provide and the email address used for delivery. Your card number and payment credentials are collected by the payment provider, never by us: we never see them and never store them.

4. What we never do

  • We do not sell, rent or trade your data. Not to anyone, in any form.
  • We place no advertising tracker and no third-party analytics script on this site. Its content security policy forbids loading any external script, which makes that promise verifiable rather than declarative.
  • We do not use your documents, your dossiers or your deliverables to train artificial intelligence models, and our AI providers undertake contractually not to do so either.
  • We do not read your dossiers. Our staff accesses them only at your request, for support, or where the law requires it — and each such access is logged.

6. Where your data is hosted

The platform database and the documents you upload are hosted in the European Union, in the Paris region (Supabase, eu-west-3). That choice is deliberate: it places dossiers under a protective and stable legal framework, close to the standards expected by our clients' partners.

Some processing takes place outside that zone — AI providers and certain payment providers in particular. Those transfers are governed by the standard contractual clauses of the European Commission or by an equivalent safeguard, and are limited to what the feature requires.

Documents handled by the CTD Builder never leave your machine, so no transfer arises for them.

7. How long we keep it

  • Account, organisation, dossiers and documents: for the life of the contract. On termination, you have thirty days to export them; we then delete them within ninety days.
  • Orders, receipts and invoices: for the period required by accounting and tax law, which may reach ten years.
  • One-off deliverables and the source documents supplied for them: twelve months, so that a delivery can be re-sent or a dispute settled, then deletion.
  • Technical and security logs: up to twelve months.
  • Demo requests and commercial contacts: three years from the last contact, or immediately on request.

8. Your rights

You have the right to access your data, to have it corrected, to have it deleted, to restrict or object to its processing, and to receive it in a portable format. Where a processing operation rests on your consent, you may withdraw that consent at any time, without affecting what was done before.

These rights arise from the Digital Code of the Republic of Benin (Law No 2017-20) and, where you are located in the European Union, from the General Data Protection Regulation.

To exercise them, write to contact@pharnos.com. We reply within thirty days. We may ask you for proof of identity where the request concerns sensitive data and doubt remains as to who is making it.

If our answer does not satisfy you, you may lodge a complaint with the Personal Data Protection Authority of Benin (APDP) or, in the European Union, with the supervisory authority of your country of residence.

9. Cookies and local storage

This showcase site sets no advertising cookie and no analytics cookie. It stores a single item locally, in your browser: your language preference, French or English. Nothing leaves your machine, and you may erase it at any time by clearing your browser data.

The platform and the CTD Builder use strictly necessary cookies and local storage to keep you signed in and to let the builder work offline. They are technical, not advertising.

The payment page displayed at checkout is served by our payment provider and follows that provider's own policy.

10. Security

All exchanges with our services are encrypted in transit. Access to the platform data is compartmentalised by organisation, at the database level, so that a request can only ever reach the data of the organisation making it. Files are stored in a private space that no public link exposes.

Administrative access is restricted to the people who need it, and is logged. Secrets never leave the server side.

Should a breach occur that is likely to affect your rights, we will notify you and the competent authority without undue delay.

11. Children

Pharnos is a professional service, not intended for minors. We do not knowingly collect data relating to a person under eighteen. If you believe that we hold such data, write to us and we will delete it.

12. Changes and contact

We may update this policy, in particular when a provider changes or a feature is added. The version in force is the one published on this page, with its version number and date. Any substantial change is notified by email to account holders.

Any question, any request: contact@pharnos.com. See also our Terms of Use and Sale and our Refund Policy.
