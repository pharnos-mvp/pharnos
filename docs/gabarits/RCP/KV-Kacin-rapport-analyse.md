## 4. Findings that need a decision

- 🔴 **Another product's name appears in your SmPC.** Page 32 of the source carries the running
  header "Product Name: **AMBIPHAR 500** / Amikacin Sulfate Injection USP 500mg/2ml", while every
  other page reads KV-Kacin 500. This is copy-paste residue from another dossier. An assessor who
  finds two product names in one SmPC questions the document control of the whole submission.
- 🔴 **Section 2 contains a batch manufacturing formula, not a unit composition.** The source table
  carries "Amount/Batch (kg)" and "Reason for Inclusion" columns, 27.500 kg of amikacin sulfate and
  "Qs to 110 lit" of water. That is a Module 3 (3.2.P.1) table. Section 2 of an SmPC states the
  composition **per unit dose** — which is what the upgraded document now does.
- 🔴 **Three adverse reactions are filed under the wrong System Organ Class.** Neurotoxicity,
  neuromuscular blockade, headache and shaking appear under *Immune system disorders*; dizziness
  under *Gastrointestinal disorders*; hypomagnesaemia under *Cardiac disorders*. **We kept your
  groupings unchanged** — reclassifying safety data is the holder's decision, not ours — but they
  should be corrected before filing.
- 🟠 **Excipient inconsistency.** Section 4.3 lists "sulfuric acid 25% (pH regulator)" among the
  excipients, but it does not appear in the list of excipients. Either the list is incomplete or the
  contraindication is wrong.
- 🟠 **The SmPC carries its parent dossier's furniture**: "Module I Administrative Information"
  headers, "1.3 Product Information" and "1.3.1 Summary of Product Characteristics" numbering, and
  page numbers 29 to 43. A standalone SmPC should carry none of it.
- 🟡 **Section 7 holds the MAH only**, as the maquette requires. No manufacturer is named anywhere in
  the source — the Notice (section 6) and the cover letter will both require it.
- 🟡 Sections 8 and 9 are marked "---" and section 10 is empty. Consistent with a first application;
  to be confirmed against your regulatory activity.

## 5. General knowledge — to be verified systematically

- **Frequency attribution.** The source states that ototoxicity, vestibular/renal toxicity and
  neuromuscular blockade are "very common **in impaired renal patients**, patients with ototoxic and
  nephrotoxic treatments and patients treated long-time". Carrying "Very common" into the general
  frequency table may overstate the risk for the overall population. The frequencies should come
  from the PSUR or the manufacturer's safety database.
- **Fertility (4.6).** No data in the source. Where the absence of data is established, the standard
  QRD wording is "No data available on the effects of KV-KACIN 500 on fertility." — to be confirmed
  by the manufacturer, not assumed.
- **Excipient with known effect.** Sodium metabisulphite was carried into section 2 from your own
  section 4.4 statement. Whether the parabens also require a statement is for your expert to decide.
