## SUMMARY OF PRODUCT CHARACTERISTICS

### 1. NAME OF THE MEDICINAL PRODUCT

GYNORIL, pessary.

### 2. QUALITATIVE AND QUANTITATIVE COMPOSITION

Each pessary contains:

- Neomycin sulfate ............ 35,000 IU
- Polymyxin B sulfate ......... 35,000 IU
- Nystatin .................... 100,000 IU
- Metronidazole ............... 200 mg

### 4. CLINICAL PARTICULARS

#### 4.1. Therapeutic indications

GYNORIL is indicated for:

- vaginal trichomoniasis;
- vaginal leucorrhoea;
- mixed vaginal infections (fungal or bacterial).

#### 4.2. Posology and method of administration

**Posology**

One pessary at bedtime for 12 days, or as directed by the physician.

**Method of administration**

Vaginal use. The pessary is inserted into the vagina at bedtime.

#### 4.3. Contraindications

Hypersensitivity to the active substances or to any of the excipients (including related antibiotic
groups).

#### 4.4. Special warnings and precautions for use

Treatment must be discontinued in the event of local intolerance or allergic reactions.

Sensitisation caused by the local application of antibiotics may compromise the systemic
administration of the same antibiotic, or the future use of other medicinal products of the same or
related groups.

The medicinal product must not be used with condoms or latex (risk of rupture).

In general, the use of spermicidal agents is not recommended during this treatment, as local
antibiotic therapy may be cancelled out by the contraceptive effect of the local spermicide.

The duration of treatment must be limited because of the risk of developing resistant strains of
micro-organisms, which may lead to secondary infections.

The risk of systemic adverse effects cannot be excluded, particularly in cases of renal impairment,
as insufficient information is available on the extent of systemic absorption of neomycin and
polymyxin B through the vaginal mucosa.

#### 4.5. Interaction with other medicinal products and other forms of interaction

Owing to the method of administration, which does not allow significant systemic absorption of the
therapeutic agents, no interaction with other medicinal products is expected.

#### 4.6. Fertility, pregnancy and lactation

**Pregnancy**

No specific testing has been carried out on the safety of the medicinal product during pregnancy in
humans. Taking medicinal products during pregnancy is not recommended.

**Breast-feeding**

Owing to the lack of data on the excretion of the medicinal product in breast milk, taking the
medicinal product during breast-feeding is not recommended.

#### 4.7. Effects on ability to drive and use machines

No effect on the ability to drive and use machines has been observed.

#### 4.8. Undesirable effects

The following adverse reactions have been reported, by system organ class:

**Skin and subcutaneous tissue disorders**

Allergic dermatitis, more frequent with prolonged use.

**General disorders and administration site conditions**

Allergic contact dermatitis, hypersensitivity reactions such as anaphylactic shock and urticaria.

Systemic adverse reactions due to the active substances of the medicinal product are not expected.

**Reporting of suspected adverse reactions**

Reporting suspected adverse reactions after authorisation of the medicinal product is important. It
allows continued monitoring of the benefit/risk balance of the medicinal product. Healthcare
professionals are asked to report any suspected adverse reactions via the national reporting system:
Agence béninoise du Médicament et des autres produits de Santé – e-mail: vigilances.abmed@gouv.bj.

#### 4.9. Overdose

No case of overdose has been reported.

### 5. PHARMACOLOGICAL PROPERTIES

#### 5.1. Pharmacodynamic properties

Pharmacotherapeutic group: Gynaecological anti-infectives and antiseptics, antibiotics,
ATC code: [Not provided, to be completed].

**Mechanism of action**

Metronidazole, a nitroimidazole, is active against various anaerobic bacteria and protozoa.

Nystatin is a polyene antibiotic with antifungal activity.

Neomycin is an aminoglycoside. It is bactericidal and active against certain gram-positive and
gram-negative organisms.

Polymyxin is active against gram-negative organisms, including *Pseudomonas aeruginosa*.
